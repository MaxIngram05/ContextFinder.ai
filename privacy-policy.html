ContextFinder.ai

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2025-01-01  ·  Last updated: 2025-01-01

◈

Short version: ContextFinder.ai collects only aggregate, anonymous usage counts — the total number of users and the number of requests made. We do not collect your name, email, browsing history, PDF contents, or any personally identifiable information. Your API key is stored locally on your device and never transmitted to us.

01

Who We Are

ContextFinder.ai is a Chrome extension that summarizes academic PDFs and answers questions about their content using AI. The extension is developed and maintained by Maximilian Ingram.

02

What We Collect

We collect only the following anonymous, aggregate data:

Data point What it is Personally identifiable?
user_count Total number of distinct extension installs that have made at least one request NO
request_count Total number of summarize or Q&A requests processed across all users NO
requests_per_user A per-install request counter used to enforce freemium usage limits. Stored as an anonymous install ID mapped to a count — no account, name, or email is ever associated with it. NO

No PDF content, page text, questions, summaries, IP addresses, or any other personal data is ever transmitted to or stored by ContextFinder.ai's servers.

03

04

Your API Key

ContextFinder.ai requires you to provide your own Anthropic API key to function. This key is stored exclusively in chrome.storage.sync on your local device and synced by Chrome across your own signed-in devices. The key is sent directly from your browser to Anthropic's API — it is never routed through, logged by, or accessible to ContextFinder.ai's servers.

05

Third-Party Services

When you use the extension, your PDF content or page text is sent directly to Anthropic's API to generate summaries and answers. This transmission is governed by Anthropic's Privacy Policy. ContextFinder.ai has no control over how Anthropic processes this data.

For scanned PDFs that cannot be parsed by PDF.js, content may optionally be sent to the OCR.space API for text extraction, governed by their respective privacy policy.

06

Data Retention

Aggregate usage counts (total users, total requests, per-install request count) are retained indefinitely for the purpose of operating the freemium usage limits. As these counts contain no personal data, there is nothing to delete on a per-user basis. If you uninstall the extension, your local chrome.storage.sync data is cleared by Chrome.

07

Children's Privacy

ContextFinder.ai is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

08

Changes to This Policy

If we materially change what data we collect, we will update the effective date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify users via the Chrome Web Store listing. Continued use of the extension after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
